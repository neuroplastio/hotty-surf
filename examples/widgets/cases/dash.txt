Widgets from outside (examples/widgets/gauge): a gauge, a callout, a
sparkline and a tagline, which both modes draw alike. The cells draw the
gauge's bar and the sparkline's strokes by blocks of their own
(Node.Cells, surfui.Graphic), the rest by what they know: the text. A click on the gauge's button
fills it ten more; a click on the callout runs its handler.
