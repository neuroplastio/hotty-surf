# 2026-11-17: a surface embedded natively in hottyterm (L4)

Branch `l4`, after its merge into `v07`. L4's third item was to embed a
surface in a program that owns its terminal. Two examples do it:
`examples/pane` (surf, cells and hottyterm alone, in raw mode) and
`surftea/examples/files` (Bubble Tea, through surftea's Host). Their
tests drive them on hottytest's host and in cells. This entry is the run
on a real host: hottyterm 26.11.16-dev, hotty 0.8 (hotty-blitz 0.8.6).

## How it ran

- A private headless Sway display (hotty-blitz's `scripts/headless.sh`,
  under this leg's own cache), the memory cap and the idle CPUs, and
  hottyterm in an isolated D-Bus session with
  `--gtk-single-instance=false`.
- The keys came from hottyterm's `scripts/feel/blackbox.py`. On a
  headless display a window has the keyboard only while a virtual
  keyboard exists, so `wtype`, whose keyboard lives for one call, reached
  nothing; blackbox holds one keyboard for its whole script. It ran its
  steps untimed, with `--jitter-ms 0`: its timing report at the end fails
  on the headless output's refresh of 0, after every step was sent.
- A shot 2 s into each 2.5 s step: start, `j`, Tab and "hi there", Tab and
  Enter, Tab, `k`, `j`, then `q`.

## What the shots show

Both programs, the same at every step:

1. The program's list on the left and the surface on the right, drawn by
   the host, at notes.md.
2. `j` moves the program's list, and the surface shows budget.csv: the
   app's state moved by the program, the surface updated by a delta.
3. Tab gives the surface the keyboard (Surface.Focus): the input has the
   focus ring, and "hi there", typed into the host, is in it. surftea's
   list dims while the surface has the keyboard.
4. Tab moves to Star inside the surface; Enter clicks it: "Starred 1
   times."
5. Tab past Star, the surface's last element, gives the keyboard back:
   no ring, and the program's keys work again.
6. `k` shows notes.md as it was, and `j` budget.csv with its note and its
   star: the state is the app's, kept across the surface's redraws.
7. `q` quits; both programs exited 0, with nothing on stderr and no hotty
   warning or error in hottyterm's log.

The shots are in `.shots/embed/` (not committed).
