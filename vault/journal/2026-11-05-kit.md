# 2026-11-05: the kit to the design board

Design Agent builds the design's hotty legs (H1 and on), each as one
commit on `design/2026-11-05-kit`, in the order its Migration board
gives. The loop is `make look` (scripts/look, README there). It draws a
story in Chromium and in Blitz, draws the board's element in Chromium
with the design's kit.css, and compares the two. The gate's numbers
below come from this sandbox with IBM Plex Sans installed. Without
Plex, 168 cases on main differ from the baseline, because the stack
falls back to Inter.

## H1: tokens, and the tooltip in a bar's tip's look

- **One canvas.** `--u-canvas` is the ground mixed 72% toward black (94%
  on light). It is the canvas behind cards and the chrome on it, so
  `--u-chrome` goes and `.u-chrome` uses `--u-canvas`. `--u-on-solid`
  (the primary button's ink) is the canvas too, as the kit has it.
- **New tokens:**
  - `--u-float`: the canvas at 96%, white at 96% on light;
  - `--u-accent-soft`: the accent at 16%, 12% on light;
  - `--u-accent-strong`: the accent at 35%, 28% on light.

  Only the tip uses `--u-float` so far. The two accent fills wait for the
  legs that use them (H3's keyboard row, H4's segmented control).
- **The tooltip** (`.u-tip`) has the float ground, the input's hairline
  and radius 6 in every density. It doesn't mix its own muted any
  more: it takes the page's, as the kit does. In Compact and Roomy it
  has 3/8 px of padding and an 18 px line. Its text is 500, a muted run
  in it 400, and two lines sit 3 px apart with 6 px above and below
  them. Its rows sit in its middle when the host's surface (whole rows)
  is taller.
- **tip/shown** shows the board's tips at the width the cells give a
  host (`cells.DrawTip`'s cols): a text that wraps, "Agents shown", and
  a host's two lines. The gate's baseline is written again for
  tip/shown only. Its new and worse cases are the 1.6× inset hairline
  (the input's line is stronger than the border's was) and Roomy's text
  sitting low, both on blitz-gaps.md already.

What it shows besides: a host's tip is as wide as its cells' card, so
it is wider than the board's. In Dense, a line that just fits its cells
wraps, in both engines. I haven't verified why; the font's advance
being a little wider than the cell would explain it. Both come from the
cells' sizing, not from CSS.

## H2: the chrome pill and the icon button

- **`pill` on a button** (surfui `Node.Pill`) makes it a chrome control,
  a control on a bar or a stripe. It is a pill with no fill at rest and
  its label muted, 500 at 13px. In Compact and Roomy it is 28px tall
  with 12px across; `small` makes it 22px with 10px across and 12.5px
  type. In Dense it is a row tall. A button of its icon alone is a
  circle as tall, the icon button (class `u-iconbtn`), with a 16px glyph
  (14 when small). An icon after a pill's label is a caret: 14px,
  muted, 4px into the padding (`u-after`, which IconButton now puts on
  an icon after its label).
- **States:**
  - the pointer on it: the hover fill and the ink;
  - `selected` (what it opens shows): the selected fill;
  - **`current`** (new, surfui `Node.Current`, `aria-current`; what it
    opens holds the keyboard): `--u-accent-soft` and the accent's ink;
  - the keyboard on it: the focus-line;
  - disabled: dim.

  **`raised`** (surfui `Node.Raised`) is a bar's picker: the selected
  fill and the ink, the input's under the pointer. A pill keeps
  `u-btn`, so the cells draw it as the button it is (Q4's lean: the
  cells keep their idiom).
- **Not drawn:** the board's press state, since `:active` isn't proven
  and the gate can't force it. Also the picker's host dot: a button
  holds one icon, and the caret is that one.
- **Stories** pill/states and pill/icon draw the board's Chrome pill and
  Icon button rows. Their 22 failing cases are on the baseline. They
  are the text's place, and a focus-line on a circle that Blitz breaks
  into dashes at 1.6× (new on blitz-gaps.md).

## H3: the keyboard's row

A focused list's selection (`.u-list:focus-within > .u-item.is-sel`) is
now the accent's soft fill, `--u-accent-soft`, with its 2px marker,
where it used to be the selected fill with the marker. In Compact and
Roomy it has no hairline: its soft fill is the accent's, as the kit has
it. It applies to every list, so a list that drew its own keyboard row
needs no override now. The new story list/states draws the
board's hosts list: a row at rest, under the pointer, selected, and the
keyboard's row. Only its cases came on the baseline (12, each a known
gap). Every other story with a keyboard row (list/keyboard, look/*,
dialog/box) stays within its baseline score.

Next to the board, these are left as they are, since no H leg names
them:
- a list row's text is 14px where the kit's is 13;
- a dot sits in a 16px slot where the kit has 6px and the row's gap;
- a selected row keeps its hairline in Compact, where the kit's has
  none.

## H4: the segmented control

`segmented` on a row of buttons (surfui `Node.Segmented`, class
`u-seg`) draws them as one control, for a scope or a filter of two to
four values. It is a sunk pill (the field's ground, the border's
hairline, 2px inside and between). Each button in it is 24px, with
12.5px type at 500, muted. The `selected` one is raised on the selected
fill with the border's hairline. The pointer turns a button's text to
the ink, and the keyboard shows its focus-line. It isn't a widget the
runtime drives, as Cycle is: each button keeps its own `@click`, and
Tab reaches each one, so the cells draw the row of buttons as they are.
Past four values, a Select (Q9's lean). The story segmented/states
draws the board's row, with the board's switches beside it; its 10
failing cases are on the baseline (the text's place).

## H5: one menu, and a host program's at a point

- **The look** (`.u-menu`): raised, radius 8, the border's hairline and
  the raised shadow. In Compact and Roomy the rows have 4px of room
  round them and are 1px apart. Each row is 28px with 8px across, its
  icon muted and 16px wide, its label 13px and its keys at its end. The
  pointer or the keyboard on a row gives it the selected fill and puts
  its icon in the ink. A heading is a caption, 600 at 11.5px, muted. A
  rule has 4px clear above and below. An action that destroys
  (`danger`) is in the error's colour, and one that can't be picked is
  dim. In Dense the rows are a cell, as a Select's open list's are.
  `--u-shadow-raised` is now the kit's two shadows, `0 1px 2px` and
  `0 6px 16px -6px`. select/open stays within its baseline score.
- **surfui:**
  - `MenuCard(from, items, hi)`, the root of a menu's surface in the
    theme and density nearest `from`;
  - `MenuID`, and `MenuItemID(i)` for row i;
  - `MenuItem.Danger` and `.Heading`.
- **surfrun:**
  - `Surface.MenuView(m, hi)` draws an element's menu (from `Menu`);
  - `OpenMenu(items, hi, MenuLook)` is a menu a host program opens at a
    point of its own (a pane's, a tab's), in a theme, density and key
    style, sized in the host's cells.

  A `MenuView` has `HTML`, `Cols`×`Rows` and `Cells`. Its rows come
  from the look's px in Compact and Roomy (at least 220px across), and
  from the cells less one in Dense, as a tooltip's do. `Place(c, r,
  cols, rows)` puts it under the point and to its right, flipped where
  the grid has no room, and kept in the grid. `Item(target)` is the item
  a press in it picks. `surfrun.MenuItem` has `Danger` and `Heading` too.
- **Markup:** `<action danger>` (or `:danger`), in both modes. The
  conformance fixtures' Dismiss is one.
- **The cells** draw a menu card as a Select's open list: a line round
  it and a row an item.
- **Story** menu/shown is the board's pane menu. Its 14 failing cases
  are on the baseline. Its keys are still boxed caps: borderless keys
  are H7's.

Left: a heading in a template's `<menu>`. Go has `MenuItem.Heading`;
the markup has no word for it yet.

## H6: one bold, and the radii as tokens

- **One bold.** `.u-bold` and a Dense heading are 600, not 700: 600
  for emphasis, 500 for labels, 400 for text. `Bold` and `Semibold`
  are one weight now. Their names stay, since templates use both.
- **The radius table** is tokens on `.u-surface`:

  | Token | Size | For |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | `--u-r-xs` | 4 | a cap, a badge |
  | `--u-r-sm` | 6 | a row, a control, a tip |
  | `--u-r-md` | 8 | a menu, a box, a query |
  | `--u-r-lg` | 10 | |
  | `--u-r-xl` | 12 | a dialog |
  | `--u-r-pill` | 999 | a switch, a pill, a segmented control |

  `--u-radius` and `--u-r` read them (`--u-r` is xs in Dense, sm
  elsewhere). No rule writes a radius of its own but `50%` for a
  circle. `--u-r-lg` has no user here: the kit's card of 10 is plx's
  pane and tool card, not surf's settings card, which stays a box's 8.
- **No radius moved a pixel.** The bold did: box/scroll's and
  cycle/states' Roomy cases sit up to 43 px² further off (the text's
  place, with Plex's SemiBold in place of its Bold), and two Compact
  cycle/states cases pass now. Their baseline is written again for
  those two stories only.

## H7, the part no open question holds

The board's H7 is one mech leg: `u-count`, `u-wg`, `u-strip`, `u-pop`,
`u-grid`, the warn-soft and error-soft roles, and borderless keys. Three
of those pieces hang on questions that are still open: the count's
name (Q11), the widget's face and its popover (Q16), and attention's
colour (Q19). Each of those gets a commit of its own, so that a
different answer costs one commit. This commit is the rest:
- **`--u-warn-soft` and `--u-error-soft`**: the warn at 14% (12% on
  light) and the error at 16% (10%). Nothing draws them yet; attention
  (Q19) does.
- **Borderless keys.** A key is a muted glyph run, no box, 12px in
  Compact, 5px from the next. This follows the maintainer's call on the
  tooltip's caps (2026-11-05: "too claustrophobic inside. Too many
  borders"). `cap` on a `<key>` (surfui `Node.Cap`) keeps the boxed
  cap, for a sheet of keys. key/caps is that sheet now. The cells keep
  drawing caps (their idiom, Q4's lean).
- **`strip` on a row** (surfui `Node.Strip`): a flat edge's strip on
  the canvas, 40px tall, items 8px from its edges. Its three rows are
  its zones: start, centre (its items in the middle) and end (at the
  end), a third each, items 4px apart. It is flex, not the kit's grid,
  since `display: grid` isn't proven.
- **`grid` on a row** (surfui `Node.Grid`): a picker's grid. Its
  buttons are 32px squares that wrap, 2px apart, an icon or an emoji at
  18px in the ink. The pointer gives a square the hover fill; the
  `selected` one gets the accent's soft fill and line.
- **Stories:** strip/zones (180 cells: a picker, tabs, tools) and
  grid/picker (icons and emoji). The borderless keys took key/styles',
  look/agents' and menu/shown's cap cases off the baseline. The baseline
  is written again for those five stories only.

## H7, Q11: a tool's count

Built to Q11's lean, which names it "count" and leaves "badge" to a
pane's corner marks, and to Q12's, which makes its tone the accent by
default. A different answer to either costs this commit alone.
- `surfui.Count(n)` is a small pill 16px tall, at least as wide, with
  its number 600 at 10.5px. Its fill is the accent, or the warn, the

- `surfui.CountDot()` is an 8px dot, something new with no number.
- `Node.WithCount(c)` puts one on a button's corner: absolute, 3px

- The story count/states draws the board's six counts on a strip's

- Left: markup. A template can't put a count on a button yet; plx's

## H7, Q16: a widget's face, and a popover

Built to Q16's lean: a widget is a face the program draws from what it
is given (an icon, a value, a unit, a tone, a meter), so every widget
matches and the cells come free. Its popover is a surface, as a tool's
is. If the plugin drew its own widget instead, this commit goes.
- `surfui.Widget(glyph, value, unit)` is a capsule on the hover ground,
  28px tall and 10px across on a host. Its icon is muted, its value 500
  in the ink, its unit 400 muted, each 6px from the next.
  - the pointer on it gives the selected fill;
  - `Selected` (its popover shows) gives the accent's soft fill;
  - `Tone` colours its icon and value (OK its icon only);
  - `Stale` dims it;
  - `Meter(f)` adds a 28×4 bar on the input, filled in the muted.

  It is a Row, so the cells draw it as one.
- `Node.Popover()` makes a Box or a Stack a popover's card: the page's
  ground, radius 10, the input's hairline and the raised shadow, what
  it holds clipped. It is the root of what a widget or a top strip's
  tool opens, in a surface of its own.
- **Stories:** widget/states is the board's faces on a strip's canvas,
  140 cells wide. At 100 cells its Dense rows overflowed, and the
  engines placed the overflow differently. pop/shown is a popover with a
  header and a list. Their failing cases are on the baseline.
- **Left:** the sparkline (an SVG polyline: a stroke in a 10× viewBox
  needs `vector-effect`, which Blitz lacks) and markup for both.

## Markup, Q11: a tool's count on a `<button>`

The third run writes the markup for what H5 and H7 left in Go. The
count first, by Q11's lean and Q12's, as the Go is, so that another
answer costs this commit and the Go one:
- `count` (an int) puts `Count(n)` on the button's corner, none at 0
  or less, as the tool clears it;
- `count-dot` puts `CountDot()` where there is no count;
- `count-tone` is its tone: `accent` (the default), `warn`, `error` or
  `muted`. Another word is a load error for a literal, a render error

They apply in that order, after `pill`, so a button of its icon alone
is still a circle (a count is no text). `surfui.CountOf` finds a
button's count, for the tone. The conformance fixtures' hide button
(frame.surf, case embed) carries one in the warn: the number added
while the inset shows, a dot while it is hidden, so the diff swaps one
for the other. Nothing draws differently: the gate is as known.

## Markup, Q16: a widget's face, and a popover

By Q16's lean, as the Go is: a widget is a face the program draws from
what it is given. If the plugin drew its own widget, this commit goes
with e627e91.
- **`<face>`** is a new tag, of a new form (`face`): its arguments
  `icon` (one of MDI's names, checked at load where it is a literal; a
  bound one it refuses is the help box and a render error), `value` and
  `unit`, each left out where empty, and nothing but `<bind>`s inside.
  It builds `surfui.Widget` through `surf.NewFace` in both modes, and
  takes `click` and `focus`.
- **`meter`** (an int, a percentage, 0 to 100) adds `Meter`, and
  **`stale`** dims it. `tone` and `selected` are every widget's
  already.
- **`popover`** on a `box` or a `stack` makes it a popover's card
  (`Node.Popover`).

The tag is `face`, not `widget`: in hotty-surf's docs every tag is a
widget of the vocabulary (`surf.Widget`, the spec's Widgets table), so
`<widget>` would read as any of them.

The conformance fixtures: shelf.surf's head has a face (the panels
counted, ok, a meter a quarter each, stale at none; case dyn-state),
and info-panel.surf's box is a popover while it is open (cases panel
and dyn-state, which toggle it). The gallery's widget stories have a
`face` too. Nothing else draws differently: the gate is as known.

Left: the cells draw a face's glyph, value and unit with no room
between them ("󰧾2panels"), where a host has 6px. That is surfui's
Widget (H7, Q16), not the markup.

## Markup: a heading in a template's `<menu>`

H5 left this: Go had `MenuItem.Heading`, the markup no word for it.
Now a `<heading>` among a `<menu>`'s items is a caption over the items
after it, its text with `{{ }}`, `:if`, `:else-if` and `:else`, no
other attribute and no element. It is an item like the others: its id
is its place in the menu, and a pick of it runs nothing. A `<heading>`
anywhere else is still the widget. Both modes build it with
`surf.MenuHeading`, and the cells and `Surface.MenuView` drew headings
already. No open question holds it.

The conformance fixtures' note row opens its menu with
`<heading>Note {{ note.id }}</heading>`, so its other items' ids move up
by one (TestMenus). Menus draw nothing in the tree, so no case's
document or cells change, and the gate is as known.

## The error on a selected row, by the maintainer's answer

The look question (in dark the selected fill, #595a5e on its ground,
left the error red at about 2.3:1) is answered: "Selected-row red
≥4.5:1", for selected rows only. So `--u-error-on-sel` is #ffc4c0 on
dark (4.56:1 on #595a5e), and the error itself on light and paper. A
selected item's error tone takes it, its dot included; every other row
keeps the terminal's red. The story list/error draws a failed machine
at rest and selected.

## Q16 answered: a widget's two paths

The maintainer answered Q16 on 2026-11-05: as a tool does, a widget
uses the SDK's built-in face or renders its own, and data-first is the
default, so a prototype touches no styling. So the face is no longer
"the program draws only from what it is given", and the commits that
made it stay:
- the data-first path is as it was: `Widget(glyph, value, unit)`, or
  `<face icon value unit>` with `tone`, `meter`, `stale` and
  `selected`;
- the custom path is the face's children: `Widget(…, kids...)`, and a
  `<face>` holds widgets now, after its icon, value and unit or in
  their place. The capsule, its states, its tone and its meter stay the
  kit's, so a custom widget still sits on a strip like the others;
- the conformance fixtures' shelf has a second face of its own dot and
  text (case dyn-state), in both modes;
- the story widget/own draws a face of dots at rest, under the
  pointer, with its popover shown, and one that mixes an icon and a
  value with a dot of its own.

`<face value="x">y</face>` was a load error, and is now a face with a
text in it.
