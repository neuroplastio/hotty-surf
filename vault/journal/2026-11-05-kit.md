# 2026-11-05: the kit to plx-design's board

Design Agent builds the design's hotty legs (H1 and on), each as one
commit on `design/2026-11-05-kit`, in the order its Migration board
gives. The loop is `make look` (scripts/look, README there). It draws a
story in Chromium and in Blitz, draws the board's element in Chromium
with the design's kit.css, and compares the two. The gate's numbers
below come from this sandbox with IBM Plex Sans installed. Without
Plex, 168 cases on main differ from the baseline, because the stack
falls back to Inter.

## H1: tokens, and the tooltip in a bar's tip's look

- **One canvas.** `--u-canvas` is the ground mixed 72% toward black (94%
  on light). It is the canvas behind cards and the chrome on it, so
  `--u-chrome` goes and `.u-chrome` uses `--u-canvas`. `--u-on-solid`
  (the primary button's ink) is the canvas too, as the kit has it.
- **New tokens:**
  - `--u-float`: the canvas at 96%, white at 96% on light;
  - `--u-accent-soft`: the accent at 16%, 12% on light;
  - `--u-accent-strong`: the accent at 35%, 28% on light.

  Only the tip uses `--u-float` so far. The two accent fills wait for the
  legs that use them (H3's keyboard row, H4's segmented control).
- **The tooltip** (`.u-tip`) has the float ground, the input's hairline
  and radius 6 in every density. It doesn't mix its own muted any
  more: it takes the page's, as the kit does. In Compact and Roomy it
  has 3/8 px of padding and an 18 px line. Its text is 500, a muted run
  in it 400, and two lines sit 3 px apart with 6 px above and below
  them. Its rows sit in its middle when the host's surface (whole rows)
  is taller.
- **tip/shown** shows the board's tips at the width the cells give a
  host (`cells.DrawTip`'s cols): a text that wraps, "Agents shown", and
  a host's two lines. The gate's baseline is written again for
  tip/shown only. Its new and worse cases are the 1.6× inset hairline
  (the input's line is stronger than the border's was) and Roomy's text
  sitting low, both on blitz-gaps.md already.

What it shows besides: a host's tip is as wide as its cells' card, so
it is wider than the board's. In Dense, a line that just fits its cells
wraps, in both engines. I haven't verified why; the font's advance
being a little wider than the cell would explain it. Both come from the
cells' sizing, not from CSS.

## H2: the chrome pill and the icon button

- **`pill` on a button** (surfui `Node.Pill`) makes it a chrome control,
  a control on a bar or a stripe. It is a pill with no fill at rest and
  its label muted, 500 at 13px. In Compact and Roomy it is 28px tall
  with 12px across; `small` makes it 22px with 10px across and 12.5px
  type. In Dense it is a row tall. A button of its icon alone is a
  circle as tall, the icon button (class `u-iconbtn`), with a 16px glyph
  (14 when small). An icon after a pill's label is a caret: 14px,
  muted, 4px into the padding (`u-after`, which IconButton now puts on
  an icon after its label).
- **States:**
  - the pointer on it: the hover fill and the ink;
  - `selected` (what it opens shows): the selected fill;
  - **`current`** (new, surfui `Node.Current`, `aria-current`; what it
    opens holds the keyboard): `--u-accent-soft` and the accent's ink;
  - the keyboard on it: the focus-line;
  - disabled: dim.

  **`raised`** (surfui `Node.Raised`) is a bar's picker: the selected
  fill and the ink, the input's under the pointer. A pill keeps
  `u-btn`, so the cells draw it as the button it is (Q4's lean: the
  cells keep their idiom).
- **Not drawn:** the board's press state, since `:active` isn't proven
  and the gate can't force it. Also the picker's host dot: a button
  holds one icon, and the caret is that one.
- **Stories** pill/states and pill/icon draw the board's Chrome pill and
  Icon button rows. Their 22 failing cases are on the baseline. They
  are the text's place, and a focus-line on a circle that Blitz breaks
  into dashes at 1.6× (new on blitz-gaps.md).

## H3: the keyboard's row

A focused list's selection (`.u-list:focus-within > .u-item.is-sel`) is
now the accent's soft fill, `--u-accent-soft`, with its 2px marker,
where it used to be the selected fill with the marker. In Compact and
Roomy it has no hairline: its soft fill is the accent's, as the kit has
it. It applies to every list, so a list that drew its own keyboard row
needs no override now. The new story list/states draws the
board's hosts list: a row at rest, under the pointer, selected, and the
keyboard's row. Only its cases came on the baseline (12, each a known
gap). Every other story with a keyboard row (list/keyboard, look/*,
dialog/box) stays within its baseline score.

Next to the board, these are left as they are, since no H leg names
them:
- a list row's text is 14px where the kit's is 13;
- a dot sits in a 16px slot where the kit has 6px and the row's gap;
- a selected row keeps its hairline in Compact, where the kit's has
  none.
