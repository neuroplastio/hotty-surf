# 2026-11-05: fit-start

A footer's hint line (`[J] next  [K] prev  [C] comment …`) is read in
order, the least important last. In a narrow pane a plain `row grow`
cuts every hint with …, and `<row fit>` drops whole children but runs
right to left, flush right, so its hints come out reversed. Not a board
item: a request from the framework's owner.

## What changed

- **`<row fit-start>`**, a bool on `row` beside `fit`: its children left
  to right from its start, each whole, those it has no room for left out
  from the last. It takes the room left and is never narrower than its
  widest child, as `fit`. `fit` is unchanged.
- **Vocabulary:** `fit-start` in the attribute table (`ModFitStart`),
  `surfui.Node.FitStart`, the interpreter's `applyBool`; the spec's
  table is regenerated, and the refused-attribute lists in the cases
  and `surf_test.go` name it. The generator and surflua read the same
  table: nothing else changed there.
- **Host CSS:** `.u-row.u-fit-start`, `.u-fit`'s rule with
  `flex-direction: row`: what doesn't fit wraps to the clipped second
  line, gone whole.
- **cells:** `fFitStart` and `placeFitStart`, `placeFit` from the other
  end. The flag type is now `uint64`: its 32 bits were used up.
- **Docs:** `docs/surf.md`'s attribute table and its note on what gives
  way.

## Where the task was silent

- **No conformance case.** None exists for `fit`; the fixtures are
  Go-hosted, and a case that brings its own templates is skipped in
  Go's modes, so a case would mean a row in a fixture, which rewrites
  every case's expected document. The cells test and the story, drawn
  in Blitz and Chromium by the gate, hold it instead.
- **The gate's baseline** lists nine `row-fit-start-*-1.6` shots (18 to
  31 CSS px²): Blitz and Chromium leave out the same hints and differ in
  the text's sub-pixel gap, as `row-fit`'s do. The 2x shots pass.
