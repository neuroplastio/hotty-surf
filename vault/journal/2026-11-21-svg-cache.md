# 2026-11-21: one svg cache for the process

Branch `svg-cache`, off main. Not pushed. Internal: no API change. A step
of making new blocks cheaper, beside the document and delta offers.

## Why

surf's fast parser hands an `<svg>` to x/net/html, since foreign content
has its own rules, once per distinct markup, and copies the tree after.
The cache was the parser's, so one per Surface. A new block is a new
Surface, so it parsed every icon again. The made-up pill the bench
uses, with three icons in front, spent a third of its Document on them.

## What changed

- **`svgs`, one for the process** (parse.go). It maps an `<svg>`'s
  markup to the tree x/net/html parsed. The trees are never changed:
  every render takes a copy.
- **It is bounded.** There are two generations, each of at most 256
  markups and 128 KB of them. An `<svg>` found in the older moves to the
  newer. When the newer fills, it becomes the older, so what goes unused
  for a generation is forgotten. Markup over 4 KB (a chart's, not an
  icon's) isn't kept. A tree takes about eight times its markup: a
  verifier measured 1.1 KB for an icon's and 130 KB for a 16 KB svg's.
  So the byte bound keeps the trees to about 2 MB, where 512 trees of
  16 KB, under the first count-only bound, came to about 67 MB. A chart
  whose markup changes each tick no longer fills the cache with dead
  trees.
- **Its keys are its own.** Each markup kept is a clone, at a first put
  and when it moves from the older generation to the newer. A slice of
  the render would keep the whole render; the first version cloned only
  at put, and a verifier found the promotion's slice.
- **It is locked,** since surfaces render on their own goroutines. get
  and put unlock without defer, which costs TinyGo's wasm 1.8 KB.
- **The copy goes into the parse's slabs** (`copyIn`), as the rest of
  the render's nodes do, where `clone` allocated each node and each
  attribute list. Each list has room for the id a render puts first,
  and an append past it moves the list. Strings are the kept tree's,
  which nothing changes.

## Proof

- **TestSvgsAreShared.** A new parser's pill with an icon another parser
  parsed takes 2,648 B where its first took 9,544 B. It also takes at
  most 6 allocations.
- **TestSvgCopiesAreTheirOwn.** What a render changes in its `<svg>`
  (an attribute, a name, appends past the room) reaches neither another
  render's copy nor the kept tree, and `<use>` keeps its `xlink`
  namespace.
- **TestSvgsForgetTheUnused.** An icon used in every generation stays,
  and one unused for two goes. There are never more than two
  generations' trees, nor more than two generations' bytes of markup,
  and a 6 KB chart isn't kept.
- **TestSvgKeysAreTheirOwn.** A kept key lies outside the render it was
  found in, after a first parse and after a promotion.
- **TestSvgsAcrossGoroutines,** under `-race`.
- FuzzParse and the parse tests hold the fast parser, svg copies
  included, to x/net/html.
- Each guarantee was sabotaged and caught:
  - a cache per parser again;
  - shared attribute lists;
  - never forgetting;
  - no length bound, and svgMax at 16 KB;
  - no byte bound;
  - no clone at put, and none at promotion;
  - no lock (a data race).

## Measured

Main c37ccbe (with the document and delta offers) against this branch,
fastest of four interleaved runs, under the build cap, at a load of 27
to 28. The block is a new Surface, the made-up pill with three 24-px icons
in front. Times are rough at that load; bytes and allocations are exact.

| bench | main | svg-cache |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pill with icons, Document | 42.6 µs, 46,696 B, 335 allocs | 28.9 µs, 26,148 B, 186 allocs |
| pill with icons, whole in cells | 112.9 µs, 104,410 B, 582 allocs | 90.6 µs, 83,865 B, 433 allocs |
| pill without icons, Document | 22.2 µs, 18,675 B, 166 allocs | 20.3 µs, 18,675 B, 166 allocs |
| pill without icons, whole in cells | 79.7 µs, 66,996 B, 408 allocs | 81.5 µs, 66,996 B, 408 allocs |

Without icons the bytes and allocations are the same, and the times are
the load's. In cells the Screen takes the document's nodes (the document
offer), so the icons are parsed once in the process, not once more by
the Screen.

hello.wasm under TinyGo: 2,504,013 B on main, 2,505,566 B here, so
+1,553 B. The first version added 2,947 B, most of it the two defers;
the byte bound adds a little back.
