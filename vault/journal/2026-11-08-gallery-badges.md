# 2026-11-08: badge controls in the gallery

A button with no badge offered a badge tone that could not be seen, and a
numbered badge offered a dot switch that the number takes precedence over.
The gallery now shows the tone when a badge or dot exists, and the dot
switch only without a positive count. Switching back from a count keeps
the earlier dot setting. The widget and its vocabulary are unchanged.

A gallery test follows the control transitions. In the host gallery the
dot and count were exercised in Graphite and Stone at full width; the
button story was also viewed at half width, where the controls column is
not shown. Widget stories have no separate cells view.
