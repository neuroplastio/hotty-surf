# 2026-11-10: surfmd, Markdown to prose (L12, G3)

Leg `a2ui/surfmd`. A module of its own, `neuroplast.io/hotty-surf/surfmd`
(Q-0004), beside surftea: goldmark 1.8.6, the core takes none and stays
TinyGo-clean. It replaces hotty-surf by `../` and is in the Makefile's
`SUBMODULES` (tidy, vet, lint, test, and now `gen` too, for its example).

## What changed

- **`Convert(src, opts...)`, `ConvertAST(n, src, opts...)`** give a
  `prose` of L11's nodes. Options: `HardWraps`, `GFM` (tables,
  strikethrough, task lists, GitHub's alerts), `Compact`, `Anchors(f)`,
  `Node(kind, f)`. Not yet: `Highlight`.
- **`Node`'s callback takes the source too:** `func(n ast.Node, src
  []byte, kids []*surfui.Node) *surfui.Node`, since a fenced block's lines
  are read from it. nil leaves the node to the default. (A call for the
  maintainer, the coordinator says.)
- **Alerts** are a quote whose first line is `[!KIND]`: the paragraph's
  runs after that line, and the blocks after it, are the alert's body.
- **Hard wraps:** a soft line break is a space, or a `br` with
  `HardWraps`; two trailing spaces are a `br` either way.
- **Nothing a document says is injected.** Raw HTML (block and inline) is
  text. A link or image address whose scheme isn't http, https, mailto or
  cid is no link / no source (`javascript:` and `data:` in any case); a link
  with a scheme is `external`.
- **`<surf-markdown :text hard-wraps gfm compact/>`**, registered through
  `surf.Register` by importing the package. The tag has a dash, as every
  registered widget's must; the coordinator keeps the rule (it keeps the
  registered tags clear of the vocabulary's future words) and `surf-markdown`
  (`surfmd.Tag`), a call for the maintainer. The flags are modifiers and
  `text` is last in the table, so it converts after them (they are
  `data-md-*` attributes meanwhile, gone after). `surfmd/examples/markdown`
  draws a page and a comment in both modes (its own `cmd/surfgen` links the
  package, as examples/widgets' does) and shows them alike.

## Interim forms (one function each, in convert.go)

- **Fenced and indented code:** `codeBlock`, a `Box` of a `Stack` of one
  `Text` a line, on the box's ground. L2's `code` replaces it, and `Highlight`
  arrives with it.
- **Tables:** `table`, a `Stack` of `Row`s whose cells are `Text`s as wide as
  their column's widest, the header bold; a cell is its plain text (no inline
  markup), alignment isn't drawn. L6's `table` replaces it.

## Tests

`surfmd/convert_test.go` runs a corpus (`testdata/*.md`: CommonMark's
basics, GFM's extras, a margin-like comment with hard wraps and compact, a
website-like page with alerts and a table; the options are in the file's
name) to goldens in both renderings, `.html` (what a host gets) and `.cells`
(60 columns); `options_test.go` holds HardWraps, Compact and Anchors, what
is never injected, `Node` and `ConvertAST` to their expected results;
`examples/markdown` the widget in both modes.

## Open

- A table cell with inline markup (a link, code) is its text only until
  `table` takes inlines.
- Footnotes, definition lists and the rest of GFM's extensions aren't
  converted (they are paragraphs of what the parser makes of them).
