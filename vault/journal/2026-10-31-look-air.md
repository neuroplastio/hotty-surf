# 2026-10-31: rows with air, a selection a rounded block

The maintainer, on plx's settings: "the left setting menu in hotty mode
is not what I've seen in the prototype. The prototype had rounded
blocks as menu items, but the screenshot has a rectangular area /
ribbon. I need all the niceness that gpui apps have". The cause was
185e116's whole-cell rows in Compact (R-24, item 7), which gov
withdrew.

## What changed

- On a host, Compact's and Roomy's rows have the mock's air again: 4px
  above and below a row, 6px for a nav's, and 2px between rows. Dense
  keeps whole cells. The cells keep a row a cell, because their layout
  is their own.
- A selected row in Compact and Roomy is a block with a hairline round
  its fill, tinted with the accent while its list has the keyboard.
  Hover lifts a nav row's text and an item's icon to the ink.
- A focused list's marker is a straight 2px bar inside the row, clear
  of its corners. As a band at the edge, the radius bent it into a "("
  (gov, from plx's palette shots).
- A Select's options stay a cell tall (the cells place its list). A
  dialog's Height is the least it takes on a host; dialog/box is 12 rows.
- The audit's fixes:
  - a caption's air;
  - Roomy's heading on its row and its chrome at 14px;
  - small wrapping text on 1.5 leading;
  - a dot on a top-aligned row's first line;
  - Compact fields and selects 10px across;
  - a Compact bare button 22px tall;
  - a dialog 8px in, with its captions in line with its rows.
- The List, Item, Nav and Entries docs say what the sheet draws.
