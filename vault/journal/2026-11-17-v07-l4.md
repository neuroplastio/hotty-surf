## The trials

Each was a fresh agent with the README, `help`, `-h` and `go doc` only,
doing the same five tasks: new, a todo app with a test, run with live
edits in hottyterm, shot and the page, and cells in tmux.

- **The first** got through all five, and found the traps above. The
  worst was B2: a Tailwind class outside the kit's set silently does
  nothing.
- **The second** took 12 minutes. Its new app was green on the first try,
  and state survived every Go reload. Its one bug, a stale failure after
  a revert, is fixed. What it left open is on the board.
