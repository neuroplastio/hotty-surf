# 2026-11-19: v0.7 goes to main

The maintainer decided that main takes v0.7 now, untagged, by a
fast-forward to surf-0.7, and that work happens on main from then on.
v0.7.0 is tagged when every row of `ui/coverage.tsv` is done, a `waits`
row counting as done. The review trail (pack drafts, reviews,
verifications) moves out of the repository, and `api/notes.md` keeps
the shapes and the decisions.

## The audit

Before the merge, an audit of surf-0.7 at 48fe24a against main's
promises:

- **The gates were green:** `make check`, `api-frozen` (the approved file
  and `api/next.txt` agree line for line), the vet at go.mod's floor
  (go1.26.0), the `surfparsecheck` tests and `make canary`.
- **CI had never run v0.7.** It triggers on main and on pull requests to
  main, and v0.7 had neither. Its timeout had gone from 60 minutes to
  30, while the tests take about 71 CPU-minutes with the race detector,
  and v0.6's last run on main took 26 minutes.
- **The README** still said the bodies were being written, and called ui
  "every Basecoat component" (23 of 39 are done).
- **surftea** requires hotty-surf through a placeholder that only its
  `replace ../` resolves, so an importer can't get it from main.
- **The public files cited panes** (p-NNN), review files that aren't in
  the repository, plx's gap ids and plan names, and one path on the
  maintainer's machine.
- **The board** stopped at 2026-11-17, and nothing since was journalled.
- **Coverage:** ten of the thirteen `port` rows already have every name,
  story and test that TestCoverage asks of them; tabs and toast lack one
  story each; sidebar lacks all of it; the three `new` rows are B4, not
  started.

## What changed before the merge (branch premerge)

- **CI's timeout** back to 60 minutes.
- **The README** for an untagged main: the API approved and implemented,
  v0.7.0 waiting for every component (23 of 39, linked to the coverage
  page), `go get` giving v0.6.3 with its docs at its tag, and `@main`
  giving this API, `go install …/cmd/hotty-surf@main` too.
- **surftea** said to be checkout-only, in its go.mod and the README, until
  main has a commit for its require to pin.
- **api/notes.md**, from 3460 lines to about 1900: L0 to L2's sections
  stay, and each pack after them is its reason, its final shape and what
  was decided. The file as it was, whole, is kept privately with its
  panes and reviews.
- **api/reasons.md, code comments, the board:** no pane ids, plx gap ids,
  plan names, plx file paths or review files. internal/book/AUDIT.md, a
  working list, went, and its open items are L3's on the board.
- **AGENTS.md:** work happens on main; every body is written; what a
  public file doesn't cite.
- **The board:** L2 to L6 as they stand, what landed since 11-17, the
  decisions, and "Before v0.7.0" as the one list of what the tag needs.

## Left, and why

- **The kit rules (K1 to K32)** are cited by number in code and docs and
  stated only where api/notes.md applies them. A list of them in the
  repository is a change of its own (on the board).

  them. They are history, and history isn't rewritten.
- **`go install …@main`** can be checked only once main has v0.7.
