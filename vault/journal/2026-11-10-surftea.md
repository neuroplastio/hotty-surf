# 2026-11-10: surftea, a surface in a Bubble Tea program (L14, B9)

Leg `a2ui/surftea`, item B9 of the a2ui-exit plan. No vocabulary and no
change to how anything looks.

## What changed

- **`surftea/`, a module of its own** (`neuroplast.io/hotty-surf/surftea`,
  Q-0004): Bubble Tea comes in through it, and hotty-surf's core and
  hotty-go stay as they were. It requires hotty-go v0.7.2 (hottytea
  v0.7.1, the one published; hottytest v0.7.2) and hotty-surf, which it
  replaces by `../` as hotty-go's modules do between each other. The
  Makefile's `SUBMODULES` has tidy, vet, lint and test cover it; fmt
  already walked every file.
- **`surftea.Host`** is margin's `surfHost` made general: a
  `surfrun.Surface` on a `hottytea.Session`. `New(name, model, program,
  Options)`, `Surface`/`Surfaces` for the Layout, `AfterLayout`, `Ready`,
  `Event`, `Key`, `Lend`, `Sync`, `Posted`, `Hide`, `Error`; in cells
  `Lines`, `Caret`, `CellsKey`, `CellsPaste`, `CellsClick`. Two
  differences from the proposal in the plan: `New` takes a nil program
  (`Attach(send)` gives it later, as a program is made after its model),
  and `Surfaces` is the call for a Layout, `Surface` its first element
  only.
- **The stylesheet is once per Session**: a weak map keyed by the Session
  (`sheet.go`), cleaned up with it. `Relayout(session, msg)` forgets it
  when the host lost its documents, so the next lay-out sends it ahead of
  them. A Session that is not Native yet owes it and sends nothing. Its
  hues are the first Host's.
- **Lists and menus** are surfaces of the Host: `WriteFrame`'s list and
  menu callbacks keep each popup (document, rect, z) and `Surfaces` lists
  it after the Host's own, `<name>-list` at Z+1 and `<name>-menu` at Z+2,
  presses on; deltas go out at once; a closed popup, or one replaced in the
  same frame, is `Session.Delete`d so the new document goes whole. The
  placement is the runtime's (`listPlacement`, `cells.PlaceBelow`,
  `PlaceAtPointer`) in the room of `clip`, from the surface's own rect. A
  menu takes the keyboard in `AfterLayout`, once placed. A list's scroll
  and reveal are the runtime's.
- **`PostMsg`** is a `Runner.Post` come back as a tea.Msg; `Posted` runs it
  on the program's goroutine and sends what changed (or updates the cells).

## What was learned

- The host has one keyboard. Two Hosts share no state, but the right's
  autofocus takes the keyboard from the left's, which the host tells with a
  blur, so the left's `Focused()` is then "". A test of "no shared focus"
  has to wait for that blur, and compares what each Host heard. Lending
  the keyboard on one Host writes its Focus, and the other hears a blur
  the same way.
- Enter on a focused button is the host's click, not a key the Surface
  takes: the test uses a binding.
- `hottytea` v0.7.1 is the newest; hotty-go v0.7.2 is only a floor for it
  by minimal version selection.

## Tests

`surftea/*_test.go` run a Bubble Tea program of two surfaces on a
`hottytest` host: the stylesheet once for two Hosts and again after a loss
(and not after a relayout that lost nothing), focus and loan of the
keyboard per Host, a Select's list a surface above the Select's under its
anchor and above it near the screen's edge (seen failing with the placement
cut), a menu at the pointer holding the keyboard, a posted function, and
the cells (text, click, key, caret) on a terminal that is no host. The
stylesheet test was seen failing with the once removed.
`surftea/examples/twosurfaces` is the program, tested the same.

## P-3

No new IPC: a Host writes hotty commands through the Session, and what a
hotty command carries is hotty's. Nothing of surftea's crosses a process.
