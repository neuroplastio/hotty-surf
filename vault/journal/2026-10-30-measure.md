## For the core

What the measures found, for a leg of its own (measures.md has the
numbers):

- Under Go, a compiled build links the whole interpreter, because
  `surf_gen.go`'s `surfSet` is a package-level `sync.OnceValue` built at
  init (`cmd/surfgen/gen.go:456`): about 0.8 MiB of the counter's
  module. TinyGo drops it.
- `reload.go`'s `crypto/sha256` brings `crypto/internal/fips140` into
  every program that has the package, in both modes: 48 KiB under
  TinyGo, about 0.16 MiB of Go's module.
- A builder's `ui.Surface` is Dense, a template's surface Compact
  (`ui/vocab.go:48`, `vocab.go:182`): the same component drawn both ways
  differs unless its template says `density="dense"`.
- The compiled palette's extra allocations are mostly its Go called
  from the template: the key caps' labels, functions here, and
  `hints()` four times a render, which a name for a value would save.
