# 2026-11-05: keycaps

Asked whether a keycap's letters should stay uppercase ("C", "Enter",
which a downstream app had approved) or follow the kit's design board
("c", "↵"), the maintainer answered: "lowercase pls".

## What changed

- **Case "" is lower.** The zero Style draws `ctrl+c`, `esc`, `f5`,
  `pgup`. `"lower"`, `"title"` and `"shifted"` are as they were. `IsZero`
  is unchanged.
- **`Case: "system"`** is the old default exactly. `"title"` gives the
  same capitals (`Ctrl`, `Esc`, `PgUp`, `F5`) in every case but macOS in
  glyphs, where the old default left `esc` lower and title writes `Esc`;
  so the old behaviour got its own name rather than changing title.
- **Enter is the return glyph on every system.** In glyphs, U+F0311 (MDI
  keyboard-return, macOS's already; now in `pcGlyphs` too); in text
  (cells), `↵` (U+21B5), as the arrows are. `Words` still says "enter":
  it takes a flag, so the screen reader never hears ↵, and the arrows
  are as they were. A person's own `Labels` win.
- **Tests and fixtures:** `surfkeys` and `pkg/cells` tests and their
  goldens follow (label case and Enter only); new cases for the zero
  Style, title, system, `Words` and a Label over Enter. No conformance
  case changed. `docs/guide.md` and `docs/surf.md` say the new default.

## Where the task was silent

- **The palette test** (`internal/screens`) uses the seed style, so its
  footer's Enter became the glyph, 4 cells narrower than "enter":
  `TestPaletteNarrow`'s widths moved from 30 and 20 to 26 and 18 to keep
  narrowing as it did.
- **The gate's baseline:** three `key-styles-*-roomy-2` cases are new at
  14.5 (the return glyph rasterising differently, the same layout). The
  scores of key-styles, key-caps and look-agents moved with their
  labels, mostly down; the maintainer had the whole rewrite committed.

## Commits

default, and Enter is ↵`); the baseline follows it.
