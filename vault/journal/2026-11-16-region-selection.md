# Region selection

A host can opt a surface into region and window focus with two root
attributes. Current list items use the selected and active theme roles;
hover layers over either. The cells emit corresponding roles and snap.
A duration can be supplied for background-color transitions. A gradient
hover fade and backdrop transitions remain unverified.

Needs a look: open `list/region` in the gallery, compare the two current
fills and their hovered versions, and check the window-unfocused row. The
fidelity gate forces the hover state and compares still images; it does not
show the motion or prove live hover matching.
