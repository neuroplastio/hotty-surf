## What went into v07

- **e83925f, cells knows the kit's layouts.**
  - cells reads no stylesheet, so what ui's stylesheet does for its
    components is taught in `cells/kit.go`:
    - a field's column, or its row by `data-orientation`, which gives
      the check box its gap (`[ ] Hard disks`);
    - an input group's order by `data-align`;
    - a breadcrumb's row and `›`;
    - a progress bar from `aria-valuenow`, and a skeleton;
    - a chart's bars and lines from their data, and a legend;
    - an avatar group;
    - a Command's or Combobox's filtered items and its empty text;
    - a popover's side.
  - `flex-wrap` wraps a row.
  - `SUBSET.md` lists them all.
  - **The gate as a test,** cells' `TestEveryStoryByKeyboard`, over every
    story:
    - Tab goes round every control, the focused one drawn reversed;
    - every control the app listens to takes the keyboard: by Tab, by
      the arrows in its group, or as a combobox's option by the arrows
      and Enter in its field, each checked by pressing them;
    - a clickable that isn't a button acts on Enter or Space.
  - It also passes on l2's stories (Command, Popover) with this cells.
- **16d6956, `hotty-surf book`.**
  - **What it shows:** the stories in a list, the one chosen drawn by the
    host, and beside it the same view on a cells.Screen in the book's
    process. Both renditions share one state.
  - **The pane's keys:** the pane takes the keyboard as one element, so
    every key but Tab reaches the program (SPEC §10.2) and goes to the
    Screen. Tab is the host's, so two empty elements round the pane carry
    it. Tab onto the one after is Tab in the Screen, and Autofocus gives
    the pane the keyboard back. Past the Screen's last control, the next
    Tab leaves.
  - **No new key:** plx's bases bind ctrl+n/p, alt+n/p and mod+n/p, so
    the book binds none.
  - **In a plain terminal,** run's fallback draws the book in cells. The
    pane has the class `hidden`, which cells reads and the book's CSS
    overrides for a host.
  - **Where it runs from:** in a checkout, under the reloader, as run does
    (`internal/book/hotty-surf-book`). An installed hotty-surf runs its
    own stories in-process.

## Seen on the way

- **The first try under the reloader failed.** The book rendered a story
  before run's dev handshake had set templ's string root. The pane now
  starts at the first render.
- **A story that opens a modal dialog takes the keyboard in the host's
  preview.** That is the host's modal, and the book can't keep it. Esc
  closes it, and `book -h` says so. On any other pick, the book keeps the
  keyboard on the list (Autofocus after the story's).
- **Keys sent by blackbox with no gap between them were lost.** 120 ms
  apart, every key arrived.
