# 2026-11-10: prose (L11, A1)

Leg `a2ui/prose`, item A1 of the a2ui-exit plan; L10 (links) is its own
entry. No Markdown here: `surfmd` (L12) writes these nodes.

## What changed

- **Fourteen tags:** `prose` (`compact`, `muted`), `p`, `h` (`level`,
  `anchor`), `em`, `strong`, `del`, `code`, `br`, `ul`, `ol` (`start`),
  `li` (`checked`), `quote`, `alert` (`kind`) and `img` (`src`, `alt`).
  Go: `surfui.Prose`, `.Compact()`, `Para`, `H`, `Em`, `Strong`, `Del`,
  `Code`, `Br`, `Bullets`, `Numbered`, `Li`, `Quote`, `Alert`, `Img`
  (`surfui/prose.go`). Both modes, surflua, the compiled generator
  (`NewH`, `NewNumbered`, `NewAlert`, `NewImg` for the ones whose
  constructor takes an argument the template gives as a modifier).
- **Structure, the same for both hosts.** Plain HTML: `p`, `h1`–`h6`, `ul`,
  `ol`, `blockquote`. A list item is `li > span.u-li-mark + div.u-li-body`,
  built by the nodes, not by `list-style`: Blitz draws no marker the gate
  shows, and the hang is a flex row on both hosts and in the cells.
  Numbers are computed by `Numbered`/`Start`, right-aligned to the widest
  (`--u-mw`). A task's marker is `☐`/`☑`; `checked` on an `li` is
  `ModChecked`, which on a list item makes it a task. A text among a
  block's children, or a run of inline ones, becomes a paragraph
  (`blocks`); whitespace between blocks is nothing.
- **Plain text runs.** Texts under the prose tags are `surfui.Str`, not
  `Text` (compiler's `inProse`, as for `link`).
- **Cells.** A paragraph is the `flow` kind already there for `line wrap`;
  its inline elements flatten into runs (L10's `inl`), which now also
  carry strike, the code fill and a hard break. A list item is a Top row
  of a kept marker and a body column (the hang is free); a quote and an
  alert are columns two cells in with a `▎` drawn over their rows
  (`fBar`); an alert's title is a bold flow in the kind's tone; `img` is
  `[alt]` in muted, or a zero-height spacer for no alt. A prose is at most
  80 columns (`box.maxw`, applied in `place`, `height` and `natural`) and a
  row between blocks; `muted` on it sets `plain`, so no tone inside
  overrides it. All of it lays out to its natural height: `Draw(cols, 0)`.
- **Look (host).** Reading size 15px at 1.6 in Compact and Roomy (the
  terminal's in Dense), `.u-prose-compact` the surface's; gaps 0.75rem
  (0.5rem compact; a row in Dense); heading 1.5/1.25/1.1em, 600; the
  alert's bar and title in info/ok/accent/warn/error; the quote's bar in the
  border colour; inline code on `--u-selected` in the terminal's font at
  0.9em; an image at most 20 rows. Decisions of mine, to be looked at.
- **Inline `code`** is the only `code` here, one tag, as asked: no
  language, spans, wrap or numbers. L2's block `code` and this entry in
  `vault/vocab.go`'s table will meet at merge; the inline form is the
  entry's FormKids with no attributes, and L2's block form is the one the
  merge decides by its attributes (`language`, `numbers`, …) or by being
  inside a paragraph. The role for its ground is `tonal`: L2's
  `code.ground` isn't on this branch.
- **Not done:** `h` sizes in the cells (bold only), a blank row more above
  a level 1 or 2, and loose lists (every item is tight).
- Alert icons are MDI glyphs (information-outline, lightbulb-on-outline,
  message-alert-outline, alert-outline, alert-octagon-outline), as the
  icon widget draws them.
