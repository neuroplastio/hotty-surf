# 2026-11-10: `link`, `Run.Href` and OSC 8 (L10, A2, B5)

Leg `a2ui/prose`, items A2 and B5 of the a2ui-exit plan, the first of two
(L11, prose, follows on the branch).

## What changed

- **`<link href external @follow>`**, `surfui.Link(href, kids...)`,
  `.External()`, `.OnFollow(act)`. A link is an `a` with `u-link`. Its
  `follow` event has the href as `$value` and the modifiers in `$event`
  (`Mods`, as a press's); `surfrun.Event.Kind` is `follow`. A click on a
  host, a press in the cells, or Enter and Space on the focused link
  make it: the host's `a` already reports clicks, and the cells' Space
  and Enter already press a link.
- **With no `@follow`:** `external` is a hyperlink and nothing more
  (`target="_blank"`, no press, no focus on a host, as `TakesFocus` already
  said); a `#fragment` that an element's `anchor` names has the next frame
  carry a `reveal` op at the start (the host scrolls, the cells' renderer
  reveals); a fragment no element has does nothing. `@follow` is taken
  before the scroll, so margin's links into a document it opens itself
  reach the program. `On("follow")` takes the `target` off a link: the
  program follows it, external or not.
- **`anchor`** is a modifier on every element: `ID(name)`, so `Box(name)`
  finds it in the cells with no new lookup (B6's `Box` by id), and the
  host's `id` is the target of `#name`. `h anchor` in L11 is the same.
- **`cells.Run.Href`**: a link's runs carry it, and runs split where it
  changes. The space between two words of one link is the link's (its
  underline runs on, and so does its OSC 8). `Screen.Golden` lists the
  linked runs under `links`, only when there are any, so no existing
  golden changed. Inline elements in a paragraph (`line wrap`, and `p` in
  L11) are flattened into the paragraph's runs, each with the look the
  elements around it give it (`inl` in `pkg/cells/layout.go`).
- **OSC 8.** Nothing below hotty-surf was needed: `surfrun.Paint` builds
  its SGR strings by hand, so `cells.SetLink` writes the OSC 8 round the
  runs the same way (`cells.PaintWith` too), closing it at a row's end.
  The off switch is `Screen.NoLinks()` and `surfrun.Options.NoLinks`. The
  href is made printable first (a control character or a byte past ASCII
  as `%XX`), because it can come from a peer's Markdown and must write no
  other sequence.
- **Focus in the cells:** a link takes the keyboard but an `external` one
  that nothing follows, as on a host; such a link is no hit either, so a
  screen of nothing else leaves the terminal's mouse alone (and its
  ctrl+click on an OSC 8 link).
- **The look:** underlined in the accent, on a host and in the cells; the
  keyboard on it a soft fill (`sel` in the cells). A tone or `muted` on the
  link takes over.
- **Text in a link is plain.** `surfui.Str` runs, not `Text` spans: a
  `Text` doesn't wrap on a host. The compiler marks the texts under
  `link` (and, in L11, the prose elements) as plain; the compiled mode
  writes `surfui.Str`.
- **surflua:** the event table has `mods` and `count` now (`$event`'s
  `Mods` and `Count`), which a Lua handler needs to tell a ctrl+click.
- **Gallery:** `follow` joins the widget story's methods, and a story,
  `link/inline`.
