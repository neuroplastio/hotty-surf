A row is a drag handle and a place to drop on in halves (drag
drop="halves"): a press on it starts its drag ($drag.from, its :key, at
@dragstart); the pointer over another row marks where the row would
land, a line at the other row's top (before) or its bottom (after);
the release there runs that row's @drop, with $drag.where, then the
dragged row's @dragend; and the mark goes.
