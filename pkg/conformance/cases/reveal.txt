In a run whose document scrolls, the runtime keeps a selection in
view: after the ops of an update it reveals each element that is
selected now and wasn't before, by as little as brings it into view
(a reveal with no value), in document order. ↓ and a click move the
selection, and each reveals the note it selects; a key that leaves it
where it is reveals nothing, and neither does a selection that goes,
its note dismissed. The new document's selected note is revealed too,
in an update of its own after it, which no step shows.
-- scroll --
The run lets the document scroll down.
