drag and drop go on a widget a pointer reaches, which takes an id's
events: not on a spacer, a rule or a dot. drop is bare (the whole
element), whole, halves or thirds. @dragstart and @dragend run on a drag
handle, and @drop on a place to drop on: without drag, or drop, they
don't load. $drag.from, $drag.where and $drag.cancel are a drag's
handler's arguments only, and $drag has no other names.
-- form.surf --
<surface>
  <item drop="sideways">a</item>
  <spacer drag/>
  <rule drop/>
  <item @drop="typed('x')">b</item>
  <item drop @dragstart="typed('x')">c</item>
  <button @click="typed($drag.from)">d</button>
  <item drag @dragend="typed($drag.into)">e</item>
  <text>{{ $drag.where }}</text>
  <item drag drop="thirds" @drop="typed($drag.where)" @dragend="commit($drag.from, $value)">f</item>
</surface>
