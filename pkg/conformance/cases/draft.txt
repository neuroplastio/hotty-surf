text, and tells its program the height in rows (@height) as it grows, in
the cells (a host's own growth is hotty-blitz's field-sizing). Every edit
reaches the model (@input); Enter types a newline; what sends is the
program's (ctrl+s, a bind on the field): it sends what the model has. A
value the program sets again moves nothing.
