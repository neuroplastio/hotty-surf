A palette gives a template's data named colours (docs/surf.md, "Colours
from data"). crew.surf declares coral, teal and plum; a literal hue="coral"
and a bound :hue="h.colour" put each element's text, or a dot, in a hue:
its class u-hue-<name>, which the stylesheet's palette rules (the css file)
colour from --u-hue-<name>, its dark value, its light one on a light
ground. A name no palette declares (mauve) draws the ink, as an empty one
does: the class is there and no rule answers it. The cells draw each hue in
its terminal colour (ansi-9, ansi-6; plum's, by its angle, ansi-12), and an
unknown one in the ink; teal is cells="6" where its angle says 14. A click
changes a bound hue in place: the class op.
