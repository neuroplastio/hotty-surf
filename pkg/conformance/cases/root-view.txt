A component's own template (here info-panel.surf, InfoPanel's) draws
one root, and a render that gives none is an error. So its top may be
an :if chain only where the chain ends in :else, which always draws:
this one doesn't, and is refused at its last element.
-- info-panel.surf --
<box :if="open" key="panel">
  <text>open</text>
</box>
<box :else-if="title != ''" key="panel">
  <text>{{ title }}</text>
</box>
-- typed --
A Lua host's shapes name no InfoPanel component, so its Load never
compiles this view's template and has nothing to refuse.
-- load --
