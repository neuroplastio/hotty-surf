Links in a paragraph that wraps, and on a row of their own. A link the
program follows runs its handler with the href as its value and the
modifiers held; an external link is a hyperlink (target=_blank) and no
press on the surface; a link to #usage, an anchor of the document,
scrolls to it (a reveal at the start) with no handler; a #fragment no
element has runs the handler that follows it. In the cells the same
links are underlined in the link tone, and each carries its href.
