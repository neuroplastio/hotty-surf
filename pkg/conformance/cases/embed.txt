<embed> draws a component chosen as the frame runs, one of Go's, by its
own Render, keyed: a click on its button is its own handler's. While the
frame's method gives none (null) it draws nothing, so the second embed,
whose :if holds only then, never shows. The tab is a partial whose root is a :if chain: drawn
selected while the frame is hidden, plain while it shows, and the one
whose chain holds nowhere draws nothing (frame-tab.surf). The hide
button carries a count on its corner, in the warn: the number added
while the inset shows, none at 0, and a dot while it is hidden.
