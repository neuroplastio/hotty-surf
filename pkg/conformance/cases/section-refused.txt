A section's heading is heading=: title= is a tooltip on every element.
Until 0.3, a section with a title and no heading is refused, so that a
template from before keeps its heading.
-- form.surf --
<surface>
  <section title="Agents"/>
  <section :title="'Agents'"/>
  <section heading="Agents" title="Who is working"/>
  <section :heading="'Agents'"><text>x</text></section>
</surface>
-- load --
form.surf:2:12: a <section>'s heading is heading= now, and title= its tooltip: <section heading=…>
form.surf:3:12: a <section>'s heading is heading= now, and :title= its tooltip: <section :heading=…>
