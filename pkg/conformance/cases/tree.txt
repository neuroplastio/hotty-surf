A file tree: a list whose items carry depth, branch, open and current,
which the model flattens (it draws no children of a closed branch), with a
caption between the rows, which the selection skips. The list answers the
keys of ARIA's tree itself, by the handlers its items carry: ↓ and ↑ select
by @click, across the captions; → on a closed branch asks @toggle, and ←
on an open one; ← on a closed one goes to its parent; Enter is @activate
and hears the item's key; End selects the last. A click on a branch's
chevron is its @toggle. The chevron is ▸ or ▾ in the cells, two cells
of room a level in front of the label, the open file's name bold in the
accent, and the count stays whole at the row's end while the name is cut.
