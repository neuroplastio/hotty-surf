the cells of a column are as wide as its widest (auto, measured in cells
and sent to a host as a width), the thread column fills and is cut
first, the age sits at the end under its chevron, and the document
column is there only while the tree review's flag is (the other case
drops it with its cells). The rows have a list's selection, which is the
model's: a click on a row that isn't selected picks it, a click on the
selected row activates it after its own click (@activate hears the
row's key), so does a double click, and Enter on the table does. The
foot says the rows shown ($range), and a row past the room scrolls into
view where the document scrolls (it starts with a7 selected, so a3 to a7 show).
