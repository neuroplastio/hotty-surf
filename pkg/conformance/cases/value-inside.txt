$value and $event are whole arguments of a handler's call: inside an
expression, even in parentheses or as an argument of another call, each
is a load error at its $. A '$value' in a string literal is a string,
and loads.
-- form.surf --
<surface>
  <field @input="typed($value + '!')"/>
  <field @input="typed(($value))"/>
  <field @input="typed([$value][0])"/>
  <field @change="commit(string($event), $value)"/>
  <field @change="commit('$value', $value)"/>
</surface>
