A palette is read where it is written: <hue name= dark= light= cells=/>,
or value= for one colour on both grounds, each its own element and
literal. A name is a lower-case identifier; colours are #rgb or #rrggbb,
and hex is written nowhere else; cells is 0 to 15. A palette comes once
in a file, before the root, holds <hue>s alone, and declares a name once.
-- pal-hex.surf --
<palette>
  <hue name="a" dark="red" light="#fff"/>
</palette>
-- pal-late.surf --
<text>x</text>
<palette/>
