A sign-up form: fields with a label above them, a password, a field of
several lines and a submit button. Enter in a field of one line commits
it and submits the form (SPEC V6-03): the field's change comes first,
then the form's submit, which is the program's check, in Go. It refuses
by setting each field's error, a message under the field in the error
tone that also makes it invalid, and where nothing is wrong it signs up
and clears them. The field of several lines types a line break on Enter
and never submits; it commits when it is left. The submit button
submits after the fields it left. A password is masked in the cells with
a bullet; a host's field is type=password. The cells draw the same
document: the same events, in the same order.
